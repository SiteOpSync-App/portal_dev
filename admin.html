Add each window, door or curtain wall type for the project, with its reference, phase, elevation / level and drawings. Inspectors choose from this list in the QA form, or pick Ad hoc if something isn't listed. Inactive installations are hidden from the QA form. Completed reports keep the details they were completed with.

An application claims every stage that has been ticked and isn't already awaiting a response or agreed. When the main contractor replies, tick the lines they agreed and save their response. Lines they didn't agree are offered again on the next application. Prices are frozen when an application is generated.
